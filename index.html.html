What you can donate

We accept only working items. Every working part helps us build a functional computer lab.

Must be in working condition

  • Working desktop PC Complete tower or all-in-one
  • Working computer monitor LCD/LED, 15" or larger
  • Working keyboard & mouse USB or wireless
